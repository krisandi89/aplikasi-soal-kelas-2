Kuis Bahasa Inggris

Jawablah 15 pertanyaan di bawah ini (dalam Bahasa Inggris).

Soal 1

Look at the picture. What is the action? (Mencuci tangan)

Soal 2

Look at the picture. What is the action for your teeth? (Menggosok gigi)

Soal 3

Look at the picture. What is the action? (Mencuci muka)

Soal 4

Look at the picture. What is the action for your hair? (Menyisir rambut)

Soal 5

Look at the picture. What is the action? (Memakai masker)

Soal 6

What part of the body do you use to see? (Melihat)

Soal 7

What part of the body do you use to eat and speak? (Makan dan bicara)

Soal 8

What part of the body do you use to hear? (Mendengar)

Soal 9

What part of the body do you use to walk? (Berjalan)

Soal 10

This grows on top of your head. What is it? (Di atas kepala)

Soal 11

What action do you do to make your hands clean? (Membuat tangan bersih)

Soal 12

What do you call hands that are *not* clean? (Tidak bersih)

Soal 13

What is the first step of washing hands? (Langkah pertama)

Soal 14

Look at the shape. What is this? (Segitiga)

Soal 15

Look at the shape. What is this? (Bintang)
